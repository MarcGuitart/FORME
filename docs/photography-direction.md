# FORME photography direction

## Directions considered

1. **Heritage archive:** museum, concours and period-correct event photographs. This gives the Archive historical context and a sense of provenance, but becomes too static if used throughout.
2. **Road and motorsport:** real movement, hillclimbs and rally stages. This creates immediate energy and gives Discovery a strong opening, but is not the right treatment for every car story.
3. **Intimate design study:** details, engines, bodywork and interiors alongside quieter environmental portraits. This makes The Garage feel observed and personal, though detail alone would not establish a collection.

## Selected direction

Use a documentary automotive editorial mix: actual cars in recognizable places, at events and in motion, supplemented by design details. Keep natural context and imperfect surroundings in frame. Avoid the uniform studio render treatment. Let each section have its own photographic register while sharing restrained color, generous image scale and small source credits.

- **Discovery / landing:** begin with a 911 rally car at Goodwood, a moving image with a wooded setting. The image supplies the energy; the interface stays quiet.
- **The Archive:** use the period Porsche museum Targa as a heritage image, with the year/model and image provenance identified.
- **The Garage / vehicle stories:** make the photographed car and its setting the main image. Use close detail only where it adds a new observation, as with the BMW 2002 Turbo engine image.
- **Collection:** mix city, concours, gathering, event and waterfront photographs. Crops vary by image rather than enforcing one front-three-quarter template.

## Asset and rights practice

The current images come from Wikimedia Commons file pages with an explicit public domain or Creative Commons license. The exact file page is linked beside image credits and in `photography-credits.html`. Photo captions and vehicle labels use the source's identified subject; dates are deliberately generalized when the photographed specimen's year is not stated. CSS cropping changes framing in the interface, not the source attribution. Before adding manufacturer press images or editorial publication images, verify the specific file's reuse terms; public availability alone is not permission.
